## Merge PDFs without uploading them
Drop several PDFs, put them in order, and get a single file. Everything happens on your device: your documents (contracts, invoices, IDs) are never sent to a server.

## How it works
1. Drop your PDF files.
2. Reorder them with the arrows (the first file goes at the start).
3. Click Run and download the merged PDF.

## FAQ
### Are my documents sent over the internet?
No. Merging happens locally in your browser or app.
### Are password-protected PDFs supported?
Not yet: remove the protection first with the dedicated tool.
### Is there a page limit?
No fixed limit: it mostly depends on your device's memory.
