## Encode and decode Base64
Convert text to Base64 or decode a Base64 string (even without padding or with line breaks). Standard and URL-safe variants are supported. Everything stays on your device.

## How it works
1. Paste the text or Base64 string.
2. Choose Encode or Decode.
3. Click Run and copy the result.

## FAQ
### Is Base64 encryption?
No: it is a simple encoding anyone can reverse. Do not use it to protect a secret.
### When should I use the URL-safe variant?
In a URL, a file name or a JWT: it replaces + and / with - and _.
### Do accents work?
Yes, text is encoded as UTF-8 before conversion.
