## About

Convert binary, octal, decimal, hexadecimal or any base from 2 to 36 using a 128-bit integer without floating-point precision loss.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

255 in base 10 becomes ff in base 16.

### What are the limitations?

Signed 128-bit integers only. Enter digits without 0x/0b prefixes, separators or decimal points.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
