## Format, minify and validate JSON
Paste JSON to indent it readably, compact it onto one line or just check that it is valid. On error, the line and column are shown. Your JSON (often sensitive data) stays on your device.

## How it works
1. Paste your JSON.
2. Choose Format, Minify or Validate.
3. Click Run and copy the result.

## FAQ
### Is key order preserved?
Yes, unless you enable alphabetical key sorting (Pro mode).
### Are large numbers altered?
No: integers and decimals are kept as written, with no rounding (no precision loss on a 20-digit ID). Only scientific notation is normalised (1E3 becomes 1e+3, same value).
### Is my data sent anywhere?
No, everything is processed locally.
