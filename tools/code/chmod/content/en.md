## About

Read owner, group and other permissions. Each digit combines read 4, write 2 and execute 1. No real permissions are changed.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

755 corresponds to rwxr-xr-x; 644 corresponds to rw-r--r--.

### What are the limitations?

Three octal digits, optionally prefixed with 0, or nine rwx/− characters. No setuid, setgid, sticky bits or ACLs.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
