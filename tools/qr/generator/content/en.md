## Create a free QR code, no account needed
Paste a link or text, pick the format, download. The QR code is generated on your device: the content is never sent over the internet, and it never expires (it is a static QR code).

## How it works
1. Enter the link or text to encode.
2. Pick PNG (images, print) or SVG (vector, any size).
3. Click Run, then download the image.

## FAQ
### Does the QR code expire?
No. The content is written directly into the image: no service can disable it.
### Which format should I choose?
PNG for the web and messaging apps, SVG for large-format print with no quality loss.
### Can I change the colors?
Yes, in Medium mode. Keep strong contrast between modules and background so the QR code stays scannable.
