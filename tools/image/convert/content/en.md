## Convert your images without uploading them
Go from PNG to JPG, WebP to PNG, or to BMP and GIF, in batches, right on your device. Your photos never leave your computer or phone, and the format is detected from the file's content, not its extension.

## How it works
1. Drop one or more images.
2. Pick the output format (and the quality in Medium mode for JPG).
3. Click Run, then download the converted images.

## FAQ
### Are my images sent to a server?
No. Conversion happens locally, with no network request.
### What happens to transparency?
PNG, WebP and GIF keep it. JPG and BMP have no transparency: it is replaced by a background color, white by default (adjustable in Pro mode).
### Is WebP lossy?
No, conversion to WebP is lossless: pixels are identical to the original image.
