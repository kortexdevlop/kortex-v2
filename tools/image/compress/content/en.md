## Shrink your images without uploading them
Reduce the weight of your photos and screenshots to email them, publish them on a website or free up space. Everything happens on your device: your images never leave your computer or phone. Hidden information in a photo (location, camera, date) is not kept, and the photo keeps its correct orientation.

## How it works
1. Drop one or more images.
2. Set the quality (75 suits most photos) and, in Medium mode, the maximum size.
3. Run the compression, then download the lighter images and read the saving.

## FAQ
### Are my images sent to a server?
No. Compression is done locally, with no network request.
### Can the result be heavier than before?
Yes, especially for an already optimized image. Pixels are always re-encoded to remove private metadata, even if this increases the file size.
### What happens to PNG transparency?
It is kept for PNG and WebP. A JPG has no transparency: it is replaced by white.
### Which quality should I choose?
Between 70 and 80 for photos. Lower values shrink the file further but visible artifacts appear.
