## Estimate your net salary from gross
Enter a monthly or annual gross salary to get an estimate of net pay before and after tax, with the breakdown of contributions and CSG/CRDS. The calculation runs on your device; nothing is sent.

## How it works
1. Enter the amount and choose gross to net (or net to gross), monthly or annual.
2. In Medium mode, set executive status and your withholding tax rate.
3. Read net before tax, taxable net and net after tax.

## FAQ
### Is the result exact?
It is an estimate for a French private-sector employee using 2026 employee-side rates. It ignores health insurance (mutuelle), provident plans, benefits in kind, Alsace-Moselle and special schemes: your payslip is authoritative.
### What is the difference between net before tax and taxable net?
Taxable net is the income tax base: non-deductible CSG and CRDS are not subtracted from it. Net before tax is what you receive before withholding tax.
### What does executive status change?
It adds the small APEC contribution (0.024% of salary, capped at 4 social security ceilings). Other employee contributions are the same.
