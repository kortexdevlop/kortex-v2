## About

Shift a date by calendar days or Monday-to-Friday days. The Gregorian calendar is used for all years.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

February 28, 2024 plus one day is February 29.

### What are the limitations?

Weekday mode skips weekends but does not know public holidays. The starting day is not counted. Years 0001 through 9999.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
