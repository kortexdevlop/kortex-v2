## About

Project savings with monthly contributions and a constant rate. Calculation is local and requires no account.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

1,000 at a 5% nominal rate compounded monthly for 10 years yields about 1,647.01 without contributions.

### What are the limitations?

Mathematical constant-rate simulation with end-of-month contributions, excluding fees, tax and inflation; no return is guaranteed.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
