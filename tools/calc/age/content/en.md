## About

Calculate completed years and days lived on a date you choose. The Gregorian calendar is used for all years.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

Someone born January 1, 2000 is 24 on January 1, 2024.

### What are the limitations?

The reference date is entered explicitly and does not update automatically. February 29 birthdays use February 28 in non-leap years. This is not a legal rule.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
