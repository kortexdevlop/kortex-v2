## About

Analyze an unweighted numeric series. Separate values with whitespace, newlines or semicolons. Dots and commas are accepted as decimal separators.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

1; 2; 3; 4 has a mean and median of 2.5.

### What are the limitations?

No thousands separators or weights. “1,2” means 1.2, not two values. Display rounds to two decimals.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
