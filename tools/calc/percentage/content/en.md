## About

Choose a formula to distinguish percentage points from relative change. Negative values are supported, while shares and changes require a nonzero base.

## How to use this tool

1. Choose a calculation.
2. Enter A and B according to the formula labels.
3. Run the calculation and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is 15% of 200?

Choose B% of A, enter A = 200 and B = 15. The result is 30.

### How is change calculated?

From 80 to 100, the change is (100 − 80) / 80 × 100 = 25%.

### Is data uploaded?

No. Calculations stay on your device and work offline after loading.
