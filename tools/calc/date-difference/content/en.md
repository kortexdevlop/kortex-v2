## About

Calculate calendar days between two dates. The Gregorian calendar is used for all years.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

January 1 to February 1, 2024 is 31 days.

### What are the limitations?

Identical dates yield zero, or one day in inclusive mode. Reversed dates produce a negative result. No time zones or daylight saving changes.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
