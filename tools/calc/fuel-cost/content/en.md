## About

Fuel cost uses your distance, average consumption and price per litre. Default values are examples.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

100 km at 6 L/100 km and 1.80 per litre uses 6 litres and costs 10.80.

### What are the limitations?

Other costs are added once, even for a return trip. Wear, insurance and consumption variations are not estimated.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
