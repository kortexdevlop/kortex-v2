## About

The calculator uses only the rate you enter; it does not select a legal tax rate. Results display two decimal places. Invoice line rounding may produce a different final total.

## How to use this tool

1. Choose whether your amount includes tax.
2. Enter the amount and the rate applicable to your transaction.
3. Read the net price, tax and gross price.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which formula is used?

Gross = net × (1 + rate / 100). Divide gross by that multiplier to recover net.

### Is a zero rate supported?

Yes. Tax is zero and net equals gross.

### Is the default rate tax advice?

No. Replace it with the rate applicable to your transaction.
