## Work out your BMI in seconds
The body mass index (BMI) compares your weight to your height: your weight in kilograms divided by your height in metres, squared. Enter both values to get your BMI, its WHO category and the weight range considered "normal" for your height. The calculation happens on your device: your data is sent nowhere.

## How it works
1. Enter your weight in kilograms.
2. Enter your height in centimetres.
3. Run the calculation and read your BMI, its category and the weight range.

## FAQ
### Is my data sent anywhere?
No. The calculation is local, with no network request.
### Is BMI a diagnosis?
No. It is a general reference for adults. It ignores muscle mass, age and body shape: for a health question, talk to a professional.
### What are the categories?
According to the WHO: below 18.5 underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 normal weight, 25 to 29.9 overweight, 30 and above obesity.
### Should I use BMI for a child?
No. This tool is meant for adults; for children, growth charts by age and sex are used instead.
