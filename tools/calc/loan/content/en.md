## About

This estimates a fixed-rate amortizing loan with monthly payments and a nominal annual rate entered by you. Optional insurance is based on the original principal. Fees, tax, variable rates and payment holidays are excluded; this is not an APR calculation.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter principal, nominal annual rate and term.
2. In Pro mode, optionally enter annual insurance on the initial principal.
3. Review the payment, costs and schedule.

## Frequently asked questions

### What happens at zero interest?

Principal is divided by the number of payments, plus any insurance.

### Does the schedule exactly match a lender offer?

No. Internal calculations are unrounded and displayed rows use two decimals; contractual rounding rules may differ.

### What is included in the total?

Principal, calculated interest and the insurance you enter. Other fees are excluded.
