## About

Calculate a tip and split the total equally. Calculation is local and requires no account.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

100 with a 15% tip totals 115, or 57.50 each for two people.

### What are the limitations?

Display rounds to two decimals; allocate a residual cent if necessary.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
