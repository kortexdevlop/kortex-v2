## About

Compare two products per kilogram or litre. Calculation is local and requires no account.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

3 for 500 g is 6/kg; 5 for 1,000 g is 5/kg.

### What are the limitations?

Use the same currency and comparable quantities: grams for both products, or millilitres for both.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
