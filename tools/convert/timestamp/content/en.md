## About

Unix timestamps measure time since January 1, 1970 UTC. Select seconds or milliseconds explicitly; the tool does not guess the unit.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

0 seconds yields 1970-01-01T00:00:00.000Z; −1 yields the previous second.

### What are the limitations?

Required date: YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ or with exactly .mmm. UTC only, no leap seconds. Second output floors fractional seconds.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
