## About

Convert two units of the same physical quantity. Decimal bytes (kB/MB/GB) are distinct from binary units (KiB/MiB/GiB). Temperatures are absolute readings, not differences.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

1 mile is 1.609344 km; 32 °F is 0 °C; 1 MiB is 1,048,576 bytes.

### What are the limitations?

No exchange rates, calendar months, imperial gallons or troy ounces. Incompatible units and temperatures below absolute zero are rejected. Display rounds to two decimals.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
