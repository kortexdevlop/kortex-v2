## A truly random password
This generator uses your device's cryptographic random number generator. Nothing is sent over the internet: the password only exists on your screen.

## How it works
1. Pick a length (16 characters is a good start).
2. Tick the character types you want.
3. Copy the password into your password manager.

## FAQ
### Is my password sent anywhere?
No. Everything is computed locally in your browser or app, with no network request.
### What length should I choose?
At least 16 characters for an important account, 20 or more for a master key.
### Why exclude ambiguous characters?
To avoid mixing up 0 and O, or 1, l and I when you have to type the password by hand.
