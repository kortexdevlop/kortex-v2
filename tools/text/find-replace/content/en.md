## About

Replace exact case-sensitive matches. Matches do not overlap; characters such as . and * are treated literally.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

Finding “cat” in “cat and cat” and replacing with “dog” performs two replacements.

### What are the limitations?

Regular expressions are not supported. Search text must not be empty; output larger than 10 MB is rejected.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
