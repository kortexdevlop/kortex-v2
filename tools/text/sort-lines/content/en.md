## Sort a list and remove duplicates
Paste a list (one entry per line) to sort it A to Z, Z to A, by length or reverse it, and remove duplicates with or without ignoring case. Nothing is sent online.

## How it works
1. Paste your list.
2. Choose the sort and options.
3. Click Run, then copy the result.

## FAQ
### Which occurrence is kept for duplicates?
The first one found: the original order is respected before sorting.
### Does sorting consider accents?
Sorting follows Unicode character order, so “é” sorts after “z”. Enable “Ignore case” to mix upper and lower case.
### Is there a size limit?
Only your device's memory.
