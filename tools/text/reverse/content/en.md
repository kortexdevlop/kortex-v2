## Intro
The text reverser flips a text in three ways: the letters from last to first, the word order of each line, or the line order of a list. Everything happens on your device, nothing is sent.

## Steps
1. Paste or type your text.
2. Choose what to reverse: the letters, the words or the lines.
3. Run it, then copy the result.

## FAQ
### Does my data leave my device?
No, everything is processed locally.
### What happens to a final line break?
In "lines" mode it stays at the end of the result.
### Are accents and emoji kept?
Accents are. Emoji made of several symbols may be split in "letters" mode.
