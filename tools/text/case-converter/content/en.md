## Change the case of a text
Switch a text to uppercase, lowercase, title or sentence case, or convert identifiers to camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case and CONSTANT_CASE. Everything happens on your device.

## How it works
1. Paste your text.
2. Pick the case.
3. Click Run, then copy the result.

## FAQ
### Are accents kept?
Yes. Accented letters are converted correctly (é ↔ É).
### How are words split for snake_case?
At spaces, hyphens, underscores and lower-to-upper changes (helloWorld becomes hello_world).
### Is my text sent online?
No, conversion happens locally.
