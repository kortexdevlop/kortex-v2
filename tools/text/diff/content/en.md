## See what changed between two texts
Paste two versions of a text to see, line by line, what was added, removed or left unchanged. Handy to review an edit, compare two drafts or check a contract. The comparison happens on your device: your texts are sent nowhere.

## How it works
1. Paste the original text, then the modified text.
2. In Medium mode, choose to ignore case or extra whitespace.
3. Run the comparison: added lines start with "+", removed lines with "-".

## FAQ
### Are my texts sent anywhere?
No. The comparison is local, with no network request.
### Does it compare word by word?
No, line by line: a changed line shows as a removed line followed by an added line.
### Is there a size limit?
Yes: 4,000 lines at most on each side, so the comparison stays fast on every device.
### What do the options do?
"Ignore case" treats "Hello" and "hello" as the same; "Ignore whitespace" ignores extra spaces and tabs.
