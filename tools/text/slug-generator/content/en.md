## About

A slug is a short version of a title suitable for a URL. This tool lowercases text, replaces separators and transliterates common Latin accents. It does not check whether a URL is already in use on your site.

## How to use this tool

1. Paste a title.
2. Choose a separator and whether to preserve Unicode characters.
3. Copy the slug and check uniqueness on your site.

## Frequently asked questions

### What happens to accents?

In ASCII mode, été becomes ete, œ becomes oe and ß becomes ss. Combining accent marks are removed.

### Are non-Latin languages supported?

Yes when ASCII transliteration is disabled; otherwise unsupported characters become separators.

### Can I limit length?

Yes. Pro mode limits characters without splitting a Unicode character.
