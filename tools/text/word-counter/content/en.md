## Count words and characters
Paste or type a text to instantly get the number of words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs and the estimated reading time. The text stays on your device.

## How it works
1. Paste or type your text.
2. Adjust the reading speed if needed (230 words per minute by default).
3. Click Run and read the numbers.

## FAQ
### Is my text sent anywhere?
No, counting happens locally in your browser or app.
### How are words counted?
A word is a run of characters separated by spaces or line breaks.
### What reading speed is used?
230 words per minute, an average silent reading speed. You can change it in Medium mode.
