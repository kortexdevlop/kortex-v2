## About

Deduplicate a list without sorting it: first occurrences retain their original text. Options compare without case or surrounding whitespace.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

A, B, A on three lines becomes A then B.

### What are the limitations?

Comparison is textual without fuzzy matching. Output uses LF line endings.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
