## Intro
The text repeater repeats a word, a sentence or a paragraph as many times as needed, with the separator of your choice: a space, a comma, a line break or nothing at all. Everything happens on your device, nothing is sent.

## Steps
1. Enter the text to repeat.
2. Set the number of repetitions and, if needed, the separator.
3. Run it, then copy the result.

## FAQ
### Does my data leave my device?
No, everything is processed locally.
### What is the limit?
From 1 to 1,000 repetitions, and a result of 1 MB at most.
### How do I put each repetition on a new line?
Enter a line break as the separator.
