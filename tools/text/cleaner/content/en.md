## About

Normalize pasted text: nonbreaking spaces and tabs become spaces, line edges are trimmed and line endings are normalized.

## How to use this tool

1. Enter values or paste your text.
2. Choose the relevant options and run the tool.
3. Read and copy the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What example can I try?

“hello   world” becomes “hello world”.

### What are the limitations?

Removing ZWJ/ZWNJ can alter emoji or some scripts: disable that option in Pro mode to preserve them. This tool does not sanitize HTML.

### Is my data uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device without an account or network transmission.
