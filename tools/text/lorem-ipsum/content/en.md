## About

Lorem ipsum helps test layouts before final copy is ready. Generation is deterministic: identical parameters produce identical text. This placeholder content has no editorial meaning.

## How to use this tool

1. Choose a count in words or paragraphs.
2. Adjust words per paragraph in Medium mode.
3. Generate the text and copy it into your mockup.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much text can I generate?

Up to 100 paragraphs of 200 words, or 1,000 words in word mode.

### Is the word count exact?

Yes. Words are separated by spaces and punctuation does not add words.

### Does it need an internet connection?

Not after the first load. No text is sent to a service.
