## Introduction
Kortex Store presents the 38 free Kortex tools. The catalogue, search and detail sheets share the same Rust/Dioxus sources in the browser and on desktop.
## Usage
Search for a tool or choose its universe. Review its details and declared permissions, then open its web version. On desktop, installed tools and their versions are detected from the local registry.
## Frequently asked questions
### Are my files uploaded?
No. Tools process their inputs on your device.
### Can I install a public desktop version yet?
Desktop packages are being prepared. Public installation remains closed until signatures, confirmation and secure token storage are validated.
### Can the browser see my local installations?
No. Installation detection and binary launching are available only in the desktop application.
