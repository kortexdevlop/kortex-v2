# Kortex Desk

Your workspace, your tools.

## Usage
Open the application and choose your existing tools. Files remain local.

## Availability
Early release. Account synchronization and native IPC remain in progress.

## FAQ
### Are files uploaded?
No.
### Is desktop complete?
Native builds are verified separately; distribution remains closed.
### Can I change language?
French and English are available.

## Manual account backup
Sign in on the website, then refresh the session in Account and backup. Only the listed metadata and preferences are sent when you choose to save. Restore replaces the layout after confirmation; Desk requires closing windows first. Files and results are never backed up. Automatic sync and secure native sign-in remain pending.
