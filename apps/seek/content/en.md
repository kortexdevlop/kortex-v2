## Overview
Kortex Seek finds the Kortex tool that matches what you need. You write what you want to do in your own words; Seek compares your request with the keywords, intents and presets each tool declares.
## Usage
Type your request, for example "merge several PDFs" or "create a strong password". Results appear as you type. Choose "Open": on the web the tool opens with the matching preset, on desktop it launches if it is installed.
## Frequently asked questions
### Is my request sent anywhere?
No. The search runs entirely on your device, from the catalogue of contracts; nothing is transmitted.
### Does Seek handle typos and accents?
Yes. Accents, plurals, small typos and equivalent words in French and English are taken into account.
### Does Seek use artificial intelligence?
Not yet. This version searches local data only; an AI level will only come with the planned consent and credits.
